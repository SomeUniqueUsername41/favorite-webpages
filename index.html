*Esperanto is an international auxiliary language that was devised in 1887 by Dr. Zamenhoff to be used by all nations. The language is mainly based on Latin, with influences from several European languages. It was designed to be easy to learn, especially for people who already know Latin-based languages, thanks to simple grammar and phonetic spelling. Many people say it can be learned quickly, and it’s used in books, original writing, and even radio.
